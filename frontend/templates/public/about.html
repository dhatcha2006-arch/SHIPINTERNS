Mission & Pedagogy

Cultivating Production-Ready Software Engineers

ShipInterns was established to solve the industry readiness gap. While standard curricula focus on abstract proofs, engineering enterprises demand distributed systems resilience, automated testing, code review literacy, and disciplined agile execution.

Every intern at ShipInterns is paired with an accredited senior engineering mentor from tier-1 technology firms. Work is verified through real pull requests, rigorous rubric evaluations, and daily work reports (DWR) that simulate top engineering teams.
